About The Bookmark Cafe

Not Just A Cafe. A Sanctuary For The Mind & Palate.

Tucked away near Adharwadi Jail Road in Kalyan West, The Bookmark Cafe was born out of a deep affection for timeless literature, slow sips, and soulful comfort food.

In an era of relentless hurry, we built a place where you can lose track of time. Whether you're here to dive into a captivating mystery novel, work remotely on your laptop with steaming cappuccino, engage in a spirited game of Catan with friends, or savor our famous Belgian Hot Chocolate and Burrata Pizza, our doors are open.

Bibliophile Haven

The Community Book Exchange

"Take a Book, Leave a Book." We celebrate the magic of shared stories.

1. Bring a Novel

Bring along any novel, manga, poetry collection, or non-fiction title you loved and want someone else to discover.

2. Swap on the Shelf

Place it on our designated Community Shelf and pick any other book to read during your coffee or take it home.

3. Join the Book Club

Meet fellow bibliophiles, participate in our monthly book discussions, and enjoy cozy acoustic evenings.
